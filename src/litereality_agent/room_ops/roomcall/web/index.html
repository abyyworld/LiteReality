    Show the others all of you

    Your webcam only sees your head and shoulders. Give the room a few pictures of the rest of you and everyone sees your whole body, moving your arms as you do, with your live face on top. The pictures go to this room's server to build the body; it is kept in memory only and forgotten soon after you leave.

    Turn your camera on to use it here, or upload photos (head to toe, facing the camera, a plain background helps).

      Bring a room you scanned into this call

      Send a scan from the LiteReality Scanner app, and everyone in this room moves into it: a quick version within seconds, then realistic objects as they are made (a few minutes). Objects people moved stay where they are.

      1. On the phone you scanned with: in Files, press and hold the scan's folder and choose Compress.
      2. Press Choose the scan and pick that .zip. Its room.usdz on its own works too, in plain colours (no photos).
      3. On a computer, you can also drop the .zip on this page.

      The scan goes to this room's server, which builds the room from it. Everyone in this room sees it; other rooms on this link keep theirs.
